Ready for what comes next?

Summit House is intended for people with a strong start in recovery who are ready to keep moving forward in a structured community.

Eligibility at a glance

  • At least six months of sobriety.
  • Worked the 12 Steps with a sponsor in Alcoholics Anonymous.
  • Ready to live in a structured, accountable recovery environment.
  • Committed to continued recovery and building a stable, independent life.

Important: This page is for a preliminary inquiry only. Please do not send medical records, diagnoses, detailed treatment history, Social Security numbers, or other sensitive information through this form.
